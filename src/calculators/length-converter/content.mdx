## How to use this length converter

1. **Enter the length.** Decimals are fine, such as 5.5 feet or 0.25 miles.
2. **Choose the units.** Pick the unit you're converting from and the one you want.
3. **Read the result.** The headline shows the converted value, with the conversion factor for one unit underneath. The length in meters and in feet and inches is always shown too.
4. **See every unit at once.** The table lists the same length in millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers, inches, feet, yards, miles and nautical miles.

## How the conversion works

Every unit is defined in terms of the meter, so the calculator converts in two steps:

**meters = value × meters per "from" unit**

**result = meters ÷ meters per "to" unit**

The definitions it uses are exact:

| Unit | In meters |
|---|---|
| 1 millimeter | 0.001 m |
| 1 centimeter | 0.01 m |
| 1 kilometer | 1,000 m |
| 1 inch | 0.0254 m |
| 1 foot | 0.3048 m |
| 1 yard | 0.9144 m |
| 1 mile | 1,609.344 m |
| 1 nautical mile | 1,852 m |

Because the inch is defined as exactly 2.54 centimeters, every U.S. customary length converts to metric exactly, with no rounding until the final figure is displayed.

## Worked example: 6 feet to meters

- **To meters:** 6 × 0.3048 = **1.8288 meters**, exactly.
- **In centimeters:** 1.8288 × 100 = **182.88 cm**.
- **In inches:** 6 × 12 = **72 inches**.

And the other way, 1.8 meters is 1.8 ÷ 0.0254 = 70.87 inches, or **5 ft 10.87 in**.

## Quick mental conversions

When you don't need exact figures, these rules of thumb are close:

- **Inches to centimeters:** multiply by 2.5 (exact: 2.54).
- **Feet to meters:** divide by 3.3, or multiply by 0.3 (exact: 0.3048).
- **Meters to feet:** multiply by 3.3 (exact: 3.2808).
- **Miles to kilometers:** multiply by 1.6 (exact: 1.609344).
- **Kilometers to miles:** multiply by 0.6, or by 5/8 for a closer answer (exact: 0.6214).
- **Yards and meters:** roughly the same; a meter is about 10% longer, so 100 yards is a little over 91 meters.

These are good for estimates. For building, sewing, engineering or anything where errors add up, use the exact conversion.

To convert centimeters to feet and inches by hand, divide by 2.54 to get total inches, then divide by 12. For 170 cm: 170 ÷ 2.54 = 66.93 inches; 66.93 ÷ 12 = 5 with 6.93 left over, so **5 ft 6.93 in**, or about 5 ft 7 in.

## The metric and U.S. customary systems

The **metric system** (the International System of Units, SI) builds every length from the meter with prefixes that are powers of ten: a millimeter is a thousandth of a meter and a kilometer a thousand meters. Moving between metric units is just a matter of moving the decimal point.

The **U.S. customary system** uses inches, feet, yards and miles, with less regular relationships: 12 inches to a foot, 3 feet to a yard and 1,760 yards (5,280 feet) to a mile. Since 1959, the United States and other English-speaking countries have defined these units exactly in terms of the meter, which is why conversions between the two systems are exact.

## How the meter is defined

The meter was first defined in the 1790s as one ten-millionth of the distance from the North Pole to the equator, then for most of the 20th century by a platinum-iridium bar kept near Paris. Since 1983, it has been defined by the speed of light: one meter is the distance light travels in a vacuum in exactly 1/299,792,458 of a second. Because every other unit of length is defined from the meter, that single constant ultimately fixes the inch, the foot and the mile too.

## Fractions of an inch in millimeters

Tools, hardware and lumber in the U.S. are often sized in fractions of an inch. Because 1 inch is exactly 25.4 mm, these convert exactly as well: 1/16 inch is 1.5875 mm, 1/8 inch is 3.175 mm, 1/4 inch is 6.35 mm, 3/8 inch is 9.525 mm, 1/2 inch is 12.7 mm and 3/4 inch is 19.05 mm. To convert any fraction, divide it out to a decimal (3/8 = 0.375) and multiply by 25.4.

Metric tools rarely match these sizes exactly. A 13 mm wrench is close to 1/2 inch (12.7 mm) but not the same, and using the wrong one can round off a bolt head. For nominal lumber sizes, remember that a "2 × 4" is actually about 1.5 × 3.5 inches once dried and planed.

## Converting step by step by hand

For conversions without a direct factor, chain the steps through units you know, and write the units down so they cancel:

- **Yards to centimeters:** 1 yard = 3 feet = 36 inches; 36 × 2.54 = 91.44 cm.
- **Miles to meters:** 1 mile = 5,280 feet; 5,280 × 0.3048 = 1,609.344 m.
- **Kilometers to feet:** 1 km = 1,000 m; 1,000 ÷ 0.3048 = 3,280.84 ft.

If the units on your final line aren't the ones you want, a step has been flipped: you multiplied where you should have divided.

## The survey foot

Until the end of 2022, U.S. land surveying used a slightly different foot, the **U.S. survey foot**, defined as 1200/3937 meters (about 0.3048006 m). The difference from the international foot is tiny, about 2 parts per million, or roughly 3.2 mm per mile, but it mattered for large-scale maps and state plane coordinates. NIST and other federal agencies have since retired the survey foot in favor of the international foot for new work. This converter uses the international foot.

## Very small and very large lengths

Science and engineering use the same metric prefixes far beyond everyday sizes:

- **Micrometer (µm),** one-millionth of a meter, also called a micron. A human hair is very roughly 50 to 100 µm across.
- **Nanometer (nm),** one-billionth of a meter, used for wavelengths of light and the features on computer chips.
- **Astronomical unit (au),** defined as exactly 149,597,870,700 meters, about the average distance from the Earth to the Sun.
- **Light-year,** the distance light travels in a Julian year of 365.25 days, about 9.46 trillion kilometers or 5.88 trillion miles.

These units aren't in the converter's list, but they convert from meters in the same way: divide by the unit's length in meters.

## Nautical miles

The **nautical mile** was originally based on one minute of latitude on the Earth's surface, which makes it convenient for navigation with charts. It is now defined as exactly **1,852 meters**, about 1.1508 statute miles or 6,076 feet. Speeds at sea and in the air are often given in **knots**, nautical miles per hour.

## Tips for accurate conversions

- **Don't round in the middle.** Convert first, then round the final answer to a sensible number of digits.
- **Match precision to the measurement.** If you measured to the nearest centimeter, a result to four decimal places of an inch is false precision.
- **Watch for area and volume.** Square and cubic units don't convert with the same factor: 1 square foot is 0.3048² = 0.0929 square meters, and 1 cubic foot is 0.3048³ = 0.0283 cubic meters. For flooring areas, see the [flooring calculator](/construction/flooring-calculator/).
- **Check units on plans and labels,** especially on imported products that may list metric sizes, and on old documents that may use the survey foot.

## Key terms

- **Meter:** the SI base unit of length.
- **International foot:** exactly 0.3048 meters, the foot used today.
- **Statute mile:** the ordinary mile, 5,280 feet or 1,609.344 meters.
- **Nautical mile:** exactly 1,852 meters, used at sea and in aviation.
- **Conversion factor:** the number you multiply by to change from one unit to another.
- **SI prefix:** a word such as milli, centi or kilo that multiplies a unit by a power of ten.
- **Knot:** a speed of one nautical mile per hour, about 1.15 miles per hour.
- **U.S. customary units:** the inches, feet, yards and miles used in the United States, all defined exactly in terms of the meter.
