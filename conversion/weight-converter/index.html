How the conversion works

Every unit is defined in terms of the kilogram, so the converter works in two steps:

kilograms = value × kilograms per “from” unit

result = kilograms ÷ kilograms per “to” unit

The key definition is the international pound, fixed in 1959 at exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. The other customary units follow from it:

Worked example: 70 kg in pounds

  1. 70 kg is already in kilograms.
  2. 70 ÷ 0.45359237 = 154.323583529 pounds.
  3. For pounds and ounces: 0.323583529 lb × 16 = 5.18 oz, so 70 kg is 154 lb 5.18 oz.
  4. For stone: 154.32 ÷ 14 = 11 stone with 0.32 lb left, so about 11 st 0.3 lb.

The quick rule of multiplying by 2.2 gives 154 lb, only a third of a pound off.

Ounces and grams in the kitchen

Recipes from different countries use different units, and weighing ingredients is more accurate than measuring cups. One ounce is 28.35 grams:

A fluid ounce is a measure of volume, not weight. A fluid ounce of water weighs about one ounce, but a fluid ounce of flour or honey doesn’t, so convert by weight only when the recipe gives a weight. To change the quantities themselves, the recipe scaler multiplies every ingredient at once.

Tons, tonnes and long tons

“Ton” means three different things:

  • Short ton: 2,000 lb, used in the United States for vehicles, freight and materials such as gravel. It’s 0.907 metric tons.
  • Long ton: 2,240 lb, used historically in the UK and still for some shipping. It’s 1.016 metric tons.
  • Metric ton (tonne): 1,000 kg, used almost everywhere else and in science. It’s 2,204.62 lb, or 1.102 short tons.

The difference matters for big quantities: 100 metric tons is 110.23 short tons. Construction materials in the U.S. are usually sold by the short ton; the gravel calculator converts cubic yards to tons for you.

Troy ounces for precious metals

Gold, silver, platinum and palladium are priced per troy ounce of 31.1034768 grams, heavier than the everyday avoirdupois ounce of 28.35 grams. One troy ounce is 1.0971 ordinary ounces, and one pound is 14.58 troy ounces. When you see a gold price, it’s per troy ounce; to value a piece of jewelry, weigh it in grams, divide by 31.1035 and multiply by the price and the gold purity (for example, 14-karat gold is 58.3% gold).

Quick mental conversions

  • kg to lb: multiply by 2.2. (70 kg ≈ 154 lb; exact 154.32.)
  • lb to kg: divide by 2.2, or halve and subtract 10%. (150 lb → 75 − 7.5 = 67.5 kg; exact 68.04.)
  • oz to g: multiply by 28, or by 30 for a rough figure. (4 oz ≈ 113 g.)
  • g to oz: divide by 28. (250 g ≈ 8.9 oz; exact 8.82.)
  • Stone to lb: multiply by 14.

Use exact figures for medicine doses, shipping limits and anything where small errors matter.

Everyday examples

  • Airline baggage: a 23 kg checked-bag limit is 50.71 lb, and a 50 lb limit is 22.68 kg, so the two common limits are almost the same.
  • Newborn weight: 8 lb 7 oz is 3.83 kg, and a 3.5 kg baby is 7 lb 11.46 oz.
  • Gym plates: a 20 kg plate is 44.09 lb, and a 45 lb plate is 20.41 kg.

Small metric units: milligrams and micrograms

Metric units step by factors of 1,000, which makes them easy to convert:

  • 1 kilogram (kg) = 1,000 grams
  • 1 gram (g) = 1,000 milligrams (mg)
  • 1 milligram = 1,000 micrograms (mcg or µg)

Nutrition labels and medicines use milligrams and micrograms. Mixing them up is a thousandfold error, so read labels carefully, and follow the dose your pharmacist or doctor gives rather than converting a dose yourself. A pound is 453,592.37 milligrams, which shows why small metric units are rarely mixed with pounds.

Shipping weights

Parcel carriers in the U.S. price by the pound, and most round any fraction up to the next whole pound: a box weighing 3 lb 2 oz is billed as 4 lb. International shipments are usually priced in kilograms, rounded up to the next half kilogram or kilogram. Large, light boxes may be billed by dimensional weight instead, a figure worked out from the box’s size, whichever is greater. Weigh the packed box, convert with this tool if the carrier uses the other system, and round up before you compare prices.

Getting an accurate reading

A conversion is only as good as the measurement behind it:

  • Use the tare button. Zero the scale with the empty bowl or box on it, so you weigh only the contents.
  • Weigh on a hard, level surface. Carpet and uneven floors make bathroom scales read low or inconsistently.
  • Check the scale’s unit setting. Many digital scales switch between kg, lb, lb:oz and g, and it’s easy to read the wrong one.
  • Mind the resolution. A kitchen scale that reads in 1 g steps can’t tell 28.3 g from 28.4 g. For small amounts, such as spices or coffee, a scale with 0.1 g resolution is better.

How precise should you be?

The converter shows six decimal places, which is more than most uses need. Round to suit what you’re doing: body weight to a tenth of a kilogram or the nearest pound, cooking to the nearest gram or tenth of an ounce, freight to the next whole unit. Keep extra digits until the end of a multi-step calculation, then round once, so small rounding errors don’t add up.

Mass and weight

Scientifically, the kilogram is a unit of mass, the amount of matter in an object. Weight is the force gravity exerts on that mass, measured in newtons. A 70 kg person has the same mass on the Moon but weighs about one-sixth as much there. On Earth, scales are calibrated to show mass, which is why kilograms and pounds work as everyday “weights”. The pound-force exists as a separate unit in engineering, but in daily life “pound” means the pound of mass.

Where the units come from

The pound and ounce descend from Roman units, and for centuries their size varied from country to country and even from trade to trade. In 1959 the United States, the United Kingdom and other Commonwealth countries agreed on the international pound of exactly 0.45359237 kg, which is why every conversion on this page is exact. The kilogram itself was redefined in 2019 in terms of the Planck constant, a fixed number of nature, instead of a metal cylinder kept near Paris. Day to day, nothing changed: a kilogram weighs the same as it did before.

Key terms

  • Avoirdupois: the system of pounds and ounces used for everyday weights in the U.S. and UK.
  • International pound: exactly 0.45359237 kg, agreed in 1959.
  • Stone: 14 pounds, used for body weight in the UK and Ireland.
  • Short ton / long ton: 2,000 lb (U.S.) and 2,240 lb (UK).
  • Metric ton (tonne): 1,000 kilograms.
  • Troy ounce: 31.1034768 g, used for precious metals.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert kg to lbs?

Multiply by 2.20462. A kilogram is 1 ÷ 0.45359237 = 2.20462262 pounds, so 70 kg is 70 × 2.20462 = 154.32 lb. For a quick estimate, multiply by 2.2 and you'll be within 0.25%.

How do I convert lbs to kg?

Multiply by 0.45359237, the exact definition of the pound, or divide by 2.20462. 150 pounds is 150 × 0.45359237 = 68.04 kg. A quick estimate: halve the pounds and subtract 10%.

How many grams are in an ounce?

28.349523125 grams, usually rounded to 28.35 g. An ounce is exactly one-sixteenth of a pound, and a pound is exactly 453.59237 grams. A 4 oz portion is 113.4 g and an 8 oz cup of a solid ingredient by weight is 226.8 g.

How many pounds are in a stone?

Exactly 14 pounds, or 6.35 kg. Stone is used for body weight in the UK and Ireland: someone weighing 150 lb is 10 st 10 lb, and 70 kg is 11 st 0.3 lb.

What's the difference between a ton and a tonne?

A U.S. short ton is 2,000 pounds (907.18 kg). A UK long ton is 2,240 pounds (1,016.05 kg). A metric ton, or tonne, is 1,000 kg, about 2,204.62 pounds. When a figure just says 'ton', check which one is meant.

What is a troy ounce?

The unit used for gold, silver and other precious metals: exactly 31.1034768 grams, about 9.7% heavier than an ordinary ounce of 28.35 g. A troy pound is 12 troy ounces, but in practice precious metals are quoted per troy ounce.

Is weight the same as mass?

Strictly, no. Mass is the amount of matter, measured in kilograms; weight is the force of gravity on it. On Earth's surface the two go together, so everyday scales show mass in kilograms or pounds, and this converter treats them the same way.
